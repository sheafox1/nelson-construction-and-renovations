How It Works

Design-build, from first meeting to finished home

How a project moves with Nelson Construction and Renovations.

1. Tell us about it

Share the project and your timing.

2. Planning and design

Floor plans, a feasibility study and your vision, drawn in AutoCAD by our design and planning team.

3. Price approved up front

The project price is set after the preliminary drawings, with room to adjust before you approve it.

4. Build with QC inspections

Detailed, stamped plans, then construction, with an in-house inspector checking each completed trade.
